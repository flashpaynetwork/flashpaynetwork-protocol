# Flash Whitepaper

## Vision

Money should arrive at the pace of a thought. Flash exists to make sending value feel as light as sending a message: fast enough that waiting is not part of the experience, plain enough that nobody needs a glossary first.

## Mission

Build payment tooling on Solana that hides the machinery and keeps the result. Publish what is confirmed, mark what is not, and never fill a gap with a guess.

## Principles

1. **One register.** Every external fact lives in a single record with a state: stated, absent or unconfirmed. Unconfirmed facts render inert.
2. **Links are derived.** A link exists only when its address is stated. Handles are read from the stored URL.
3. **Market data is independent.** The live board follows its own source record and is unaffected by the contract address.

## Roadmap

**Phase 1, Ground floor.** Brand, website and the live market board go public so there is something real to inspect.

**Phase 2, Address goes public.** The contract address is confirmed and appears in the pinned slot at the top of the site and in this repository.

**Phase 3, Wallet flow.** A send screen that takes a handle and an amount and shows settlement the moment it clears.

**Phase 4, Open rails.** Public repositories, tooling and integrations so other builders can wire Flash into their own products.

Stages are ordered, not scheduled. Details will change as the project learns.
